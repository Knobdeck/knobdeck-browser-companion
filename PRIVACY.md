# Privacy

The KnobDeck Browser Companion:

- **collects nothing and sends nothing.** It has no analytics, no telemetry and no crash
  reporting, and it connects to nothing but KnobDeck on your own computer (`127.0.0.1`).
- **reads only what a button needs, when KnobDeck asks:** your open tab's address and title, and
  your most recent bookmarks and history entries (16 of each, to show on a wheel). That information
  goes no further than KnobDeck on the same computer.
- **doesn't read page content.** It asks for access to all sites only so it can print a page, stop
  it loading and send Custom Keybind key events to it.
- **stores only** a random id for your browser profile and, if you set one, the port.

For KnobDeck itself see the [KnobDeck privacy policy](https://knobdeck.harshalkudale.com/legal/privacy).
