# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- First release: Chrome, Edge, Brave (and other Chromium browsers) and Firefox / Zen.
- Connects to the KnobDeck `browser-plugin` on `127.0.0.1:32147` and runs its actions through the
  browser's own extension APIs: tabs, windows, private windows, back/forward, reload, zoom, print,
  bookmark, bookmarks and history lists, open a link, browser pages (Chromium).
